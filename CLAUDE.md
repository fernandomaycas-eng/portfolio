# Portfolio — Fernando Maycas

## Objective
Premium personal portfolio for sports broadcast design, motion graphics, sports social content and audiovisual production. Prioritize complete visual systems for leagues, competitions and clubs: openers, scorebugs, player cards, lineups, lower thirds, wipes and matchday graphics. Premier League and LaLiga are quality references, not assets to copy.

## Creative direction
Initial proposal: editorial, cinematic, high-contrast, bold condensed typography. Carbon #0C0C0C, off-white #F2F2ED, graphite #282828, optional volt #C5FF00. Avoid generic SaaS layouts, excessive glow and gamer aesthetics. English-first copy. Proposed hero: SPORTS. MOTION. BROADCAST. Subtle, purposeful motion; respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Structure
Home: hero, showreel, selected work, about, contact. Work categories: Broadcast Design, Sports Social Content, Filmmaking & Editing, and 3D & CGI. Keep broadcast as the primary specialization. Sports Social Content includes Instagram Stories, vertical reels, matchday stories, score updates, sports campaigns and animated social graphics. Present vertical 9:16 work in dedicated portrait grids or campaign groups, preserving the native aspect ratio without cropping or stretching. Allow mixed aspect ratios in project data and case studies. Use clean portrait frames rather than mandatory phone mockups. Include sports social content in selected work when real assets are available. Reusable case studies: title, role, video, stills, breakdown, deliverables and tools. Candidate projects: Matchday System, Copa UNIDAVI, Playtomic, WIMU; verify details and publication rights. Clearly label conceptual work and placeholders. Never invent client claims or represent stock imagery as original work.

## Technical approach
React + Vite + TypeScript, lean CSS, optional GSAP. Responsive, accessible, keyboard-friendly. Central editable project data. Optimize videos with posters and lazy loading; no autoplay audio. Do not commit original large media, credentials or private information. Repository is public. Prepare GitHub Pages-compatible base path /portfolio/; do not deploy without approval.

## First milestone
Build a polished homepage prototype, with navigation, typographic hero, labeled showreel placeholder, featured broadcast project, selected work and footer. Verify production build. Work on a feature branch and open a pull request for review before merging. Document setup and media replacement in README.
